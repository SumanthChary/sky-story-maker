## 📊 Ad Format Comparison

### 1. **Google AdSense (RECOMMENDED) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐**

**What It Is:**
- Google automatically serves the highest-paying ads to your users
- Can show: Display banners, video ads, text ads, interactive ads
- Changes based on what pays most at that moment

**Earnings Potential:**
- **CPM**: $3-10 per 1,000 ad views
- **For 10,000 visitors**: $30-100/month
- **For 100,000 visitors**: $300-1,000/month

**Why It Pays Most:**
✅ Google has millions of advertisers bidding for your space  
✅ Real-time bidding = highest possible price

✅ Serves relevant ads based on user interests

---

### 2. **Static Image Ads ⭐⭐**

**What It Is:**
- You place a banner image (JPG, PNG)
- Links to advertiser's website
- You find sponsors yourself

**Earnings Potential:**
- **Sponsored Post**: $50-500/month (if you find a sponsor)
- **Low CPM**: $0.50-2 per 1,000 views
- **For 10,000 visitors**: $5-20/month

**Why It Pays Less:**
❌ You have to find advertisers yourself  
❌ Static images get ignored (banner blindness)  
❌ No bidding = lower prices  
❌ Manual work to update ads  

**Setup Time:** Hours to find sponsors

---

### 3. **GIF/Animated Ads ⭐⭐⭐**

**What It Is:**
- Animated GIF banner
- Catches attention better than static
- Still manually managed

**Earnings Potential:**
- **CPM**: $1-4 per 1,000 views
- **For 10,000 visitors**: $10-40/month

**Why It's Medium:**
✅ More engaging than static images  
❌ Still requires finding advertisers  
❌ Can be annoying to users  
❌ Google AdSense serves better ads automatically  

**Setup Time:** Hours + design work

### 4. **Video Ads (Manual) ⭐⭐⭐⭐**

**What It Is:**

- Platforms: Google Ad Manager, Media.net

**Earnings Potential:**
- **CPM**: $8-25 per 1,000 views
- **For 10,000 visitors**: $80-250/month
- **Requires**: Minimum 10,000 monthly visitors

**Why It Pays Well:**
✅ Highest engagement  
✅ Premium advertisers  
❌ More technical setup  
❌ Traffic requirements  
❌ Google AdSense already serves videos automatically  

**Setup Time:** 2-3 hours

## 🚀 Quick Setup for Google AdSense

### Step 1: Sign Up (10 minutes)
1. Go to [Google AdSense](https://www.google.com/adsense/)
2. Sign up with your Google account
3. Add your domain name
4. Submit for review (approval takes 1-2 days)

### Step 2: Get Your Codes (5 minutes)
Once approved:
1. **Get Publisher ID:**
   - Go to Account → Settings
   - Copy your publisher ID (format: `ca-pub-1234567890123456`)

2. **Create Ad Unit:**
   - Go to Ads → By ad unit
   - Click "Display ads"
   - Name it: "Constellation Banner"
   - Size: Responsive
   - Click "Create"
   - Copy the Ad Unit ID (10-digit number)

### Step 3: Add to Your App (5 minutes)

**A) Update index.html:**
Already added! Just replace `YOUR_PUBLISHER_ID` on line 17 with your actual ID:
```html
<script async src="https://pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle.js?client=ca-pub-YOUR_ACTUAL_ID"
     crossorigin="anonymous"></script>
```

**B) Update AdContainer.tsx:**
Already set up! Just replace on lines 21-22:
```tsx
data-ad-client="ca-pub-YOUR_ACTUAL_PUBLISHER_ID"
data-ad-slot="YOUR_ACTUAL_AD_UNIT_ID"
```

### Step 4: Test (10 minutes)
1. Publish your site
2. Wait 10-20 minutes for ads to start showing
3. Visit your site (ads won't show in preview mode)
4. You should see real ads!
